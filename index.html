    What exactly gets merged?
    • Workout history — union of all sets; a set present in several backups is kept once, and sets logged twice on purpose stay twice.
    • Exercises & categories — matched by name, ids rewired; missing ones are added.
    • Routines — copied whole when the name is new; existing routines are left untouched.
    • Body weight, measurements, goals, plates, workout notes — union, duplicates dropped.
    • Settings — always taken from the base backup.
